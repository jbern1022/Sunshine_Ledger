## 2. What the confirmed awards share (descriptive, not selection criteria)

* **Applicants were established organizations**: newsrooms, a university school, a public radio station, an
  open-data nonprofit. None of the confirmed awards went to an individual or a for-profit product company.
* **The Florida money is in collaboration**: every 2026 Press Forward South Florida award named partner outlets.
* **The closest Florida precedent is a newsroom, not a tool builder**: The Florida Trib received $75,000 for
  data tools about the Legislature's local impact. That is Sunshine Ledger's subject, delivered through a
  newsroom.
* **Legislative-data precedents are small, prototype-scale grants** ($35,000 for Access Missouri and the NJ
  legislation tracker). The large data award ($420,000) went to cross-portal infrastructure by an
  established nonprofit.

## 3. Implications to test, not conclusions

These follow from the table and need checking against each program's current published rules (Gate 6):

1. **Applicant pathway.** If current calls require nonprofit or newsroom applicants, Sunshine Ledger's realistic
   route is as a **data partner on a newsroom-led application** (Florida Trib, FIU Caplin, WLRN) or through a
   fiscal sponsor. Ticket `6hxjxVGPJx22wc6G` decides this.
2. **Partner overlap.** The Florida Trib's funded work (Legislature impact on South Florida, data tools) overlaps
   directly. Outreach should offer the site as a source for that work, not compete with it.
3. **Grant scale.** Prototype-scale ($25k–$100k) is the precedent band for a first ask; $140k/year is not.
4. **Post-enactment tracking** (NJ Spotlight's "was the law enforced") matches Milestone step 2 and the
   "post-enactment results" design ticket, a differentiator worth showing in the dossier.
